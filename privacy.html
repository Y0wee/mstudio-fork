Privacy policy

mStudio Fork is a personal app used only by its author. It has no servers and sends no data anywhere except to the services listed below, directly from the phone.

University data

After you sign in with your university account, the app reads your timetable, grades, messages and similar data from the university's own API and keeps a copy on the phone so it opens instantly offline.

Gmail

If you connect Gmail, the app gets read-only access and uses the Gmail API only to find emails sent by ClickMeeting and read the personal class-room links in them. It cannot change, send or delete email, and it does not read, store or send any other email. Google user data is used only to provide this feature, stays on the device, is never shared with third parties and is never used for advertising or to train AI models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

ClickMeeting

When you ask it to, the app submits the class registration form (first name, last name, email) to ClickMeeting on your behalf.

Removing access

Disconnect Gmail in the app settings, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.
